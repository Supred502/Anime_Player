# Privacy

Anime Player runs on your computer. There is no Anime Player server, no
account with us, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives nothing
from the app unless you choose to send a problem report yourself.

## What stays on your computer

- Your watch progress, library tabs, saved words, settings and downloads, in
  the app's own folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\AnimePlayer` on Windows,
  `~/.local/share/animeplayer` on Linux).
- Your AniList login token, and a Jimaku API key if you add one, in your
  system's password store (Windows Credential Manager, or the KDE/GNOME
  keyring).

## Who the app talks to, and why

- **AniList** — the catalogue, your lists and progress, ratings and the
  airing schedule. Only with your AniList login when you've logged in.
- **The streaming website** the videos come from, and **AniSkip** and
  **Jikan** for intro/outro times and filler lists.
- **Jimaku** — Japanese subtitles, only if you add your API key.
- **GitHub** — checking whether a new version is out.
- **Discord** — only the Discord app already running on your own computer,
  to show what you're watching on your profile. It can be turned off in
  Settings. Discord's own privacy policy covers what it does with that.
- **Your phone** — only if you use the phone remote, over your own Wi-Fi.

## Problem reports

"Report a problem" opens a GitHub issue in your browser with the app's
version, your operating system and the app's recent error messages filled
in. You see all of it before anything is posted, and nothing is sent unless
you submit it.
